# Coaching for engineering leaders

You're running an AI rollout. The metrics are mostly green. Your team has stopped pushing back. Something feels off - and you can't tell whether it's alignment, friction, or the start of attrition you won't see until it's too late.

That's the conversation this coaching exists for.

## Who this is for

- **Engineering managers, directors, and VPs** at 50-500 person tech companies
- Running an active AI tooling rollout, or about to run one
- Privately uncertain whether your team is bought in or quietly compliant
- Seeing no ROI from AI rollouts while engineers' morale is slumping.
- Looking for someone who's navigated this kind of org-level tooling shift before - and isn't going to sell you a framework PDF

This is not the right work for: pure individual coaching unrelated to a team context, generic leadership development without a tooling-transformation lens, or anyone looking for a vendor to validate an AI strategy that's already locked in.

## What an engagement looks like

A typical engagement runs **12 weeks**:

- **Weekly 1:1s** - 60 minutes, focused on what's actually happening on your team this week, not abstract leadership theory.
- **Async support between sessions** - short-form Slack or email, for the in-the-moment decisions where a 24-hour wait kills the value.
- **Targeted diagnostics** - frameworks like the [12 Signals checklist](/signals/) you can run on your team in under an hour, with a debrief in the next session.
- **Stakeholder navigation** - how to manage upward (the CTO asking for adoption metrics) and across (peer VPs, the AI champion you appointed) as the rollout matures.

Pricing is structured per-engagement, not per-session. The first conversation is a 30-minute discovery call at no cost - we both decide if the fit is real before any commitment.
